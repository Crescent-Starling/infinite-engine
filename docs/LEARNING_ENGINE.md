# Learning Engine — long-term design, not implemented

Curiosity and intrinsic interest motivate learning. Goals and activities describe different dimensions; they are not mutually exclusive learning styles or diagnoses.

| Dimension | Values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Motivation | Curiosity, intrinsic interest, user-chosen practical needs | Why the user engages; never inferred as clinical personality |
| Goal | Explore, Understand, Apply, Advance | Current intent; multiple goals may coexist and change |
| Activity | Orient, Explore, Explain, Question, Organize, Apply, Evaluate, Reflect | Actions that can serve any goal |

Optional declarative Learning Templates suggest activities with user-editable order and parameters. Users can skip, change or stop a template. Structure-first navigation and AI conversations are equal entry points into the same knowledge services; neither requires completing a prescribed sequence.

User-led co-construction distinguishes view customization (a saved projection), personal interpretation (user-authored explanation), candidate professional revision (review queue), and verified professional knowledge (approved revision). A view edit or saved explanation never modifies canonical knowledge. A candidate must retain source context, old revision and explicit reviewer decision before promotion.

Personal Cognition records questions, explanations, revisions and observable events. Future optional growth visualizations may show activity history, questions revisited and user-assessed confidence with clear limitations. Document counts, imported concepts, graph coverage and completed templates do not measure expertise. The aspiration is to help develop domain expertise through sustained reasoning and practice; the system cannot certify it from activity alone.

No forced streaks, frequent nudges or psychological diagnosis. Historical interpretations remain recoverable beside current reference knowledge. Evaluation must distinguish task performance, self-report and evidence of learning; see [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).
